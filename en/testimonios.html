Recognition

Work that leaves a mark

Beyond the classroom, Carmen’s work has been recognised by institutions, festival organisers and fellow musicians.

2013 · Aula d’Extensió Universitària

The best talk of the series

After her first talk on vocal technique, the institution chose a photograph of it for the cover of its Christmas cards and invited her to give a second talk.

2008–2023 · Choral festivals

Invited again and again

The organisers of the Kraków and Warsaw festivals have invited her onto the jury for nine editions, alongside composers such as Romuald Twardowski.

2014 · Biblioteca Clarà

Written thanks

People who attended her talk on “Vocal technique, the soloist and the choir” wrote to thank her for the questions she had answered.
