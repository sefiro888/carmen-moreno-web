Podcast · Mi técnica vocal paso a paso

Vocal technique, in her own voice

Seven episodes (in Spanish) in which Carmen explains her method out loud, from breathing to how to vocalise. An hour and a half to listen to at home, on the way to rehearsal or before practising.

“We do technique when we vocalise.”Carmen Moreno

Episode 1 · 7 episodes · 1 h 35 min Vocal technique and interpretation
0:00 / 8:43

The episodes

What you’ll learn in each one

Listen to them in order, like a short course, or go straight to the topic you’re interested in.

018:43

Vocal technique and interpretation

Why vocal technique is the foundation of interpretation: what you need to master so the piece expresses what the singer feels.

028:47

Breathing

Abdominal breathing and the support pressure that sustains long phrases, held notes and high notes.

0311:16

The jaw drop

Jaw protrusion and the jaw drop: the movement that frees the high note before it arrives.

0410:37

Placing the sound on the vomer

The ultimate goal of the vocalise: a resonant, free and bright sound, placed on the vomer.

0511:52

The paranasal sinuses

What the paranasal sinuses are and why they act as resonance chambers for the voice.

0614:53

Why vocalising matters

Why vocalising is not warming up: the vocalise as technique applied to the intervals in the pieces.

0729:08

How to vocalise

The most complete episode: how a vocalise exercise is worked on, step by step.

To get the most out of it

  • Listen with headphones to appreciate the vocal examples.
  • After each episode, try the movement with a simple exercise in front of a mirror.
  • If something doesn’t work, note it down and bring it to your first lesson with Carmen.
