The experience

Just like the studio, without leaving home

The online lesson keeps what matters most about an in-person one: Carmen sees you, hears you and corrects your movements on the spot.

Face to face

Carmen watches your breathing, jaw and lips while you vocalise, just as in the studio.

Shared score

She shares the score on screen to point to the exact interval you are working on.

Your repertoire

Exercises are chosen according to the intervals in the pieces you want to sing.

How it works

Four steps to your lesson

No accounts or passwords: you talk directly to Carmen and receive your private link for each lesson.

Request

Fill in the form: what you want to work on, when suits you and which country you’ll connect from.

Confirm

Carmen replies with the day, time and format that suit you best.

Join and sing

You receive the private video call link. At the agreed time, click and your lesson begins.

Before the school

Seven online courses that proved it

Between 2020 and 2022 Carmen taught seven weekly online courses. The results were so good that she decided to turn the experience into her own school: an effective, professional learning space, open to anyone, anywhere.

Her experience of in-person teaching goes back much further: since 1995 she has worked with professionals, students and amateur performers.

What you need

  • A space where you can sing without disturbing anyone or being disturbed.
  • A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone. Headphones help.
  • A stable internet connection.
  • The score of the piece you want to work on, if you have it.
  • A glass of water and the wish to sing.

Carmen will send you a different video call link for each lesson. Don’t share it: it’s just for you.

Frequently asked questions

About the online school

Which video call platform is used?

Carmen will send you the link to her video call room for each lesson. Just open it at the agreed time; if you need to install anything, she’ll tell you beforehand.

Does singing sound good over a video call?

Yes, with a stable connection. It’s worth testing your microphone before the first lesson. Headphones avoid echo when Carmen accompanies you or sings an example.

Can I pay from outside Europe?

Yes. When you get in touch, Carmen will send you payment options with prices in your local currency.

In which language are the lessons?

Lessons are usually in Spanish. If you’d prefer another language, mention it in your request.

Are there recorded courses?

For now, the school focuses on live lessons. In the meantime you can start with the teaching video series and the book with DVD.

Your first lesson

Tell Carmen what you want to sing and what you find difficult. She will suggest a starting point and a schedule that suits you, online or in Barcelona.
