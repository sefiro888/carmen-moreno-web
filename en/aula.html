Online classroom

Your lesson starts here

Enter the code Carmen sent you and click “Join lesson”. The video call will open in a new window.

Live lesson

Three groups of letters. You’ll find it in Carmen’s message. You can also paste the full link.

The lesson opens in Google Meet. On a computer it works in the browser, with nothing to install. On a phone it opens the free Meet app.

Before you join

Five minutes before, get ready

  • Find a place where you can sing undisturbed.
  • Keep your score and a glass of water at hand.
  • Headphones help to avoid echo.
  • Place the camera so that your face and shoulders can be seen.
  • When the browser asks, allow the camera and microphone.
  1. Enter the code and click “Join lesson”.
  2. Type your name if asked, and click “Ask to join”.
  3. Carmen lets you in and the lesson begins.

Help

If something doesn’t work

Do I need a Google account?

No. You can join with just your name. If you have a Google account, you can use it too.

It says to wait until someone lets me in

That’s normal: Carmen lets you in when the lesson starts. If a few minutes go by, message her on WhatsApp.

I can’t be seen or heard

Click the padlock next to the address in your browser and allow the camera and microphone. Then reload the page. Also close any other programs that use the camera.

I can’t find the code

It’s in Carmen’s message, inside the link: the last part, like abc-defg-hij. If you have the link, you can also just click it.

Is the lesson recorded?

Only if Carmen asks you first and you agree.
