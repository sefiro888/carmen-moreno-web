Sources and credits

Where each piece of content on this website comes from.

Texts

Her career, the method, the lesson programmes and the technical advice come from Carmen Moreno’s own writing: her previous website Mi técnica vocal paso a paso and her blog Carmencita Moreno, el Canto y la Dirección Coral (2007–2025, in Spanish). They have been rewritten and summarised for this website, and direct quotations appear in quotation marks. The dates in the calendar come from the reports on that same blog. The English and Catalan versions are translations of the Spanish texts.

The article “Vocalizing with the Choir before Singing” was published in the International Choral Bulletin of the International Federation for Choral Music (IFCM), vol. XXXIV, no. 2, 2015. Spanish edition.

Photographs

The photographs of concerts, choirs, festivals and talks belong to Carmen Moreno’s personal archive. The atmospheric scenes (curtain, stage, studio, piano and desk) are illustrations created for this website. The anatomical diagram of the method is an original, simplified drawing with no medical value.

Videos

The series Mi técnica vocal paso a paso plays from Carmen Moreno’s Vimeo account. The introduction video and the testimonials come from her previous website. The testimonials will only be published permanently with the permission of the people who appear in them.

The podcast, the 3D animations from the DVD, the book pages and the sheet “Vocal technique: tip 1” are Carmen Moreno’s own material. The illustration of a singing lesson is by her student Elizabeth Moody.

Privacy

This website does not use tracking cookies or store personal data. The forms prepare a message that you send yourself from your email or WhatsApp. Fonts are served from this website, without connecting to external services. When you play a Vimeo video, that service’s player is loaded.
