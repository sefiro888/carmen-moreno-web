About the book

Years of research, in your hands

How do you breathe? How do you let the jaw drop? Where do you place the sound? What role do the lips and their corners play? These are every singer’s questions, and this book answers them with a scientist’s precision and a soprano’s experience.

Each explanation comes with a 3D DVD that shows what happens inside the abdominal cavity and the face while singing. The book covers the vocalise and its application to repertoire, as well as topics rarely found in a manual: vocal hygiene, hormonal changes, age and the singer’s emotions.

A story with a rose

Born on Sant Jordi’s Day

On 23 April 2012, Catalonia’s day of books and roses, Carmen launched Mi técnica vocal paso a paso on Rambla de Catalunya with her editor and signed her first copies there.

The first edition was published by Parnass Ediciones. The book then moved to BALAM, which distributed it in Costa Rica, Mexico and Spain. Since 2016 Carmen has offered it directly through her website, the only sales channel she authorises.
