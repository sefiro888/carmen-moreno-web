Cookie policy

What this website stores in your browser and why.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves on your device to remember information. Similar technologies, such as the browser’s local or session storage, do similar jobs.

2. What this website uses

This website does not use its own cookies, nor analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It only keeps three technical items in your browser’s session memory, which are deleted when you close the tab and are not sent to any server:

  • cm-intro: remembers that you have already seen the entrance animation so it does not repeat.
  • cm-programa: remembers your quiz result to fill in the lesson request.
  • cm-vimeo: remembers that you accepted loading Vimeo videos, so you are not asked again during this visit.

The typefaces, the website’s own videos and the podcast are served from this same website.

3. Third-party services

  • Vimeo. Some videos play from Vimeo. A notice is shown first and they only load if you accept, with the do-not-track option enabled. Vimeo may store cookies or information on your device; see its cookie policy.
  • Google Meet and WhatsApp. When you open a video call or a chat, you leave this website and Google’s and Meta’s policies apply.
  • Other links. Carmen’s blog and other linked publications have their own cookies.

4. Why there is no cookie banner

The technical storage described above is essential for the website to work and is exempt from consent (Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE). Since no analytics or advertising cookies are used, no general banner is shown. Vimeo videos, which may store information, only load after you accept them in a notice shown first. If that changes, your consent will be requested before anything else is enabled.

5. How to delete or block cookies

You can delete or block cookies and site data from your browser’s settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.). When you close the tab, this website’s three session items disappear on their own.
