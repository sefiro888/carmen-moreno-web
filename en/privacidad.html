Privacy policy

What data is processed, why, and what your rights are.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

1. Data controller

Carmen Moreno Muñoz, Tax ID 47876160G, address: Tienda Arts Guinardó (Sala Mozart), calle Sicilia 354, 08025 Barcelona. Contact: carmen@mitecnicavocalpasoapaso.com.

2. What data we process and why

  • If you write by email, WhatsApp or through a form: your name, email or phone number and the content of your message (for example your level, your goal or your availability). To answer your enquiry and, if you wish, propose lessons or other services.
  • If you become a student or order the book: the data needed to give lessons, agree schedules and payments and, for the book, the delivery address. To provide the service and meet tax obligations.

No profiles are built and no automated decisions are made. Data is not sold or passed to third parties for advertising.

3. How your data reaches us

This website has no server of its own that collects data. The forms (lesson request and contact) and the “What is your starting point?” quiz only prepare a message: when you press the button, your email or WhatsApp app opens with the text already written, and the data only reaches the owner when you press “Send” in that app. The quiz answers only travel in that message if you decide to send it; until then, the result is only remembered in your browser during your visit.

4. Legal basis

The consent you give by writing to us and providing your data, the performance of the service or order you request (lessons, book) and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Retention

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to deal with it and afterwards for the limitation periods of any possible liability. Client data is kept for the duration of the relationship and for the periods required by tax and commercial law.

6. Providers and recipients

To operate, the owner uses third-party services that may process data on her behalf:

  • DonDominio (domain and the website’s mailbox).
  • Google (the personal email account that receives a copy of the messages, and Google Meet video calls for lessons).
  • Meta / WhatsApp (WhatsApp messages).
  • GitHub (website hosting, via GitHub Pages).
  • Vimeo (playback of some videos).

These providers have their own privacy policies. Some may be established outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States, in which case transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses).

7. Browsing and third-party services

When you visit the website, the hosting server (GitHub Pages) may record your IP address and technical browser data for its operation and security. The thumbnails of some videos are loaded from Vimeo, which means a connection to that service. Vimeo videos only load when you accept them in a notice shown first, and with the do-not-track option enabled. No analytics or advertising tools are used.

8. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to carmen@mitecnicavocalpasoapaso.com and saying which right you wish to exercise. If you believe your data is not being handled properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

9. Minors

The services on this website are aimed at people over 14. If someone under that age would like lessons, a parent or guardian must make contact.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest revision appears at the top of the page.
