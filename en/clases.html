01 · From scratch

Introduction to vocal technique

For anyone who wants to learn the basics of singing and apply them to their repertoire from the start.

What you will learn

  • Abdominal breathing: the movement of the rib and abdominal muscles that creates the support pressure for sustaining phrases, long notes and high notes.
  • Jaw protrusion and movement, and the work of the facial and lip muscles involved in the projection, colour and placement of the sound.
  • A milestone: placing the sound on the vomer, to produce a resonant, free and bright tone.

Everything is worked on during the vocalise, carefully observing how each interval is executed, because they are the same intervals you will later sing in the pieces. “Once technical skill is developed, inner feeling is empowered and gives the piece a personal, unique character.”

02 · Choirs

Technical coaching for choristers

For choir singers who need technical help to study and prepare the pieces their group is rehearsing.

Carmen has conducted choirs since 1999 and has always trained each chorister “with the same technique and vocal quality required of a soloist at a conservatoire”. In these lessons you bring your score and work together on the passages you find hard: tuning your line, leaps, long notes and entries.

  • Keep your line in tune while listening to the other voices.
  • Solve the difficult intervals in your choir’s repertoire.
  • Enjoy a choral experience that is “rewarding and great fun”.

03 · Soloists

Coaching for professional singers

For classical professionals and advanced students striving to keep improving, who want to correct “unwanted results”, whether or not they know the cause.

Pinpoint

Which technical details you already master and what causes the problems you describe.

Select

The exercises with the intervals that correct that cause.

Apply and check

Carry the understanding over to the same intervals in the piece and check how far it has come.

Sometimes a well-supported vocalise is enough. At other times the goal is to explore more vocal possibilities and secure your range and tessitura, without going beyond your own natural limits.

Also for contemporary music

Pop and contemporary performers

The technical foundation is the same as in classical singing. To stay true to the style, the lip corners are not kept as close and support pressure is less demanding: with good use of the rib muscles, the performer keeps their phrasing. If they later want to move into classical singing, they just add those details.

04 · Working voice

The voice as a working tool

For teachers, broadcasters and other professionals who talk all day and need to understand the technical basics that keep their vocal apparatus healthy.

You will learn abdominal breathing and the movements of protrusion and sound placement. That way your voice carries without straining the vocal folds, and you reach the end of the day without fatigue or hoarseness.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Not sure which programme to choose? Take the four-question test or write to Carmen and she’ll guide you.

Take the testWrite to Carmen
What do I need for an online lesson?

A space where you can sing without disturbing anyone or being disturbed, a computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, and a stable connection. If you are working on a piece, have the score to hand: Carmen can also show it to you on screen.

Can I bring my own repertoire?

Yes, and it’s recommended. Technique is worked on in the vocalise and applied to the same intervals in the pieces you are singing.

Are there lessons for groups or whole choirs?

Besides the open Sunday session, Carmen has given vocal technique workshops for chamber groups and talks at community centres and libraries. Write to her to discuss your case.

What if I can’t make a lesson?

Let Carmen know in advance so you can find another date. She will tell you the specific terms for changes and cancellations when she confirms your lesson.

Your first lesson

Your voice already knows how to sing. Let’s show it how.

Tell Carmen what you want to sing and what you find difficult. She will suggest a starting point and a schedule that suits you, online or in Barcelona.
